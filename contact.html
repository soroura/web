Say Hello

Bring a real problem.

The most useful collaborations begin with a setting where the problem matters, or expertise that changes the quality of the work.

Ways to collaborate

  • Bring a field workflow, programme, assessment, or exercise that needs a better tool.
  • Offer a team or setting for a bounded, well-documented pilot.
  • Review methods, scenarios, indicators, or operational assumptions.
  • Contribute expertise in offline systems, multilingual design, interoperability, data governance, or responsible AI.
  • Help design research that tests usability, learning, preparedness, adoption, or service outcomes.
  • Connect a useful component to an institution capable of maintaining it.

A useful first message

Tell me what you are trying to do, where the current process breaks, who experiences the problem, and what you could contribute to testing or solving it.
