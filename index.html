Dr Ahmed Sorour, Cairo

Closing the distance between what we teach and the care people receive.

TrainingPractice

I am a doctor working on health systems and emergency care at WHO Egypt. We train health workers well; what happens after the course is where my work sits. Along the way I build small tools to learn what actually helps, and I share what works and what doesn't.

See what I've built Read my story

Why I build

My aspiration is to learn by building. I make a tool, use it on real work, and watch what changes. First in the way I work. Then, if it holds up, in how health care is delivered.

AI can organise, compare, draft and suggest. People decide.

Four areas, one gap

Health systems

Primary care, hospital quality, migrant and refugee health. Two assessment platforms came out of this work.

Emergency care and preparedness

Basic Emergency Care, mass casualty management, hospital resilience. A course platform came out of this work.

Learning by building

Small digital tools, built to find out what actually helps the people doing the work. AI is one of the tools, not the point.

Evidence and data

A master's in data science, and the habit of asking what the data can and cannot tell us.
